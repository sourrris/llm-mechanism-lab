# Hardness ladder

Move upward rather than merely spending more hours.

| Level | Operation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recognize | “I have seen this term.” |
| 1 | Retrieve | Explain without notes. |
| 2 | Derive | Recover the equation and shapes. |
| 3 | Implement | Build it from a blank file. |
| 4 | Predict | State an experimental result before running. |
| 5 | Intervene | Change the mechanism and obtain the predicted behaviour. |
| 6 | Discover | Produce a new, replicated causal result. |

The sprint moves core ideas to Levels 3–5. Research standing comes from repeated Level 6 work.
