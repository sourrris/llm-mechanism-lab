## Build and train

- Stanford CS336, Language Modeling from Scratch, Spring 2026: https://cs336.stanford.edu/
- Attention Is All You Need: https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762

Use CS336 as the implementation backbone. Its assignments cover tokenizer/model/optimizer construction, systems, scaling, data and alignment/reasoning RL. This sprint deliberately compresses only the parts needed for behavioural mechanism work.

## Reading rule

Read only to answer a question generated by implementation or experiment. For each source, record:

1. What claim is made?
2. What evidence supports it?
3. Is the evidence correlational or causal?
4. What assumptions or approximation does the method introduce?
5. What experiment would transfer the idea into your own lab?
